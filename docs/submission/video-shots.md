## Finding: CARLA 0.10 on this server does not render weather or night

Weather is set (`carla_cutin._reset` calls `set_weather`, restored in `close()`), but the UE5 camera frames stay
clear noon: `video-swarm-rainnight-001` (hard_rain_night) and the older `carla-hunt-003/closest-view` (dense_fog)
both look like daylight. What the environment *does* change: LiDAR attenuation/dropoff/noise (sparser, noisier
cloud in RViz) and friction (mu 0.5 rain-night, 0.7 fog). So in the video, say "weather acts on the LiDAR and the
road friction"; never claim the picture shows night/fog. Shot 6 (clear night) dropped for this reason; shot 4
(anchored run) not re-run: it needs testnet gas, which was not authorised, and `cutin-001` is already anchored and
verified (commit `0x8ba47d24…f633a`, finalize `0x34fae933…64f6`, `verification.json`: publicRootOk, chainOk,
metricsRecomputedOk, signaturesAllTrue all true).

### Remotion edit rules

- Use the Studio's actual palette (`#0a0a0a`, `#fbfbf6`, yellow `#f3f56c`, cyan `#7cecf5`, pink `#f5a3ef`) and its Space Grotesk / JetBrains Mono pairing. CARLA remains the hero; the motion graphics connect real captures. Do not make a fictitious dashboard.
- Assign cyan to **0G decisions/Storage**, pink to **Hedera payment**, and yellow to **CARLA safety metrics**. When the current Studio already uses a color differently, preserve the UI and use these colors only in the overlaid motion layer.
- Use three recurring visual motifs: a moving road line for the scenario, one pulsing square per 0G decision, and a single payment arrow that becomes a one-run permit. This gives the viewer continuity without diagrams full of arrows.
- Cuts should follow an action: car crosses lane line → action JSON lands; payment settles → permit appears; decision rows stack → Storage root appears. Avoid long logo animations, code-scroll montages, and a full terminal screenshot.
- Keep captions under two lines; safe-area 90 px; body text at least 42 px at 1080p. Show a full transaction ID or root only at the proof hold; elsewhere use a short ID and a QR/link in the description.
- The music drops under the 0G decision exchange, rises for the swarm, and cuts for SAFE_HALT. Keep tire/brake sound below narration. Make the ElevenLabs read conversational, roughly 145–155 words/minute, with short pauses after “one run,” “0G Storage,” and “unreachable.” Render a version with burned-in subtitles and one clean version.
