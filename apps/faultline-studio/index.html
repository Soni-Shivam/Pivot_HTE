Adversarial testing for self-driving software

An AI attacker on 0G drives the other cars in a CARLA simulation and tries to make your driving policy crash; every decision and result is recorded on-chain.

Live LiDAR map · CARLA Town10

How Faultline works

  1. Your driving policy — the self-driving stack under test; it sees only its own sensors.
  2. CARLA simulator — physics, roads and a 64-beam LiDAR, stepped 20 times per second.
  3. Faultline controller — asks the attacker every 1 s, enforces safety limits, stops safely on errors.
  4. 0G Private Computer — the attacker model decides in a TEE; each answer has a request ID and signature.
  5. Evidence — 0G Storage (public + sealed log), 0G Galileo (commit before, finalize after), Hedera (x402 payment + HCS receipt), Arkiv (shareable claims).

What you get

  • Pass/fail verdict
  • Closest gap and time to collision
  • The attacker’s reasoning for each decision
  • Full replay
  • On-chain receipts
